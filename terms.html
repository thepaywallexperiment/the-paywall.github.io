These Terms & Conditions govern participation in THE PAYWALL, an online 30-day video experiment.

By purchasing a video slot, you acknowledge that you have read, understood and accepted these Terms & Conditions.

1. THE PAYWALL

THE PAYWALL is an online experimental project in which participants purchase individual video slots.

Each purchased slot allows the purchaser to submit one video for consideration and publication as part of THE PAYWALL experiment, subject to these Terms & Conditions and the project's content rules.

THE PAYWALL is intended as an internet experiment and entertainment/media project.

2. The 30-Day Experiment

THE PAYWALL operates as a limited 30-day experiment.

The experiment begins at the officially announced launch date and time and ends 30 calendar days later.

Once the experiment ends:

  • New slot purchases will no longer be accepted.
  • The final amount raised will be calculated.
  • The community allocation will be calculated.
  • The applicable community voting process will be finalized.

THE PAYWALL may display a countdown or other indication of the remaining experiment period.

3. Slot Pricing

The first available slot costs €1.

After a slot is successfully purchased, the price of the next available slot doubles.

The pricing formula is:

Price of Slot N = €1 × 2^(N - 1)

For example:

Slot #1  → €1
Slot #2  → €2
Slot #3  → €4
Slot #4  → €8
Slot #5  → €16
...

The purchase of a slot must be successfully completed before the price of the next slot is increased.

A failed, cancelled, refunded or otherwise unsuccessful payment does not constitute a completed slot purchase.

4. Number of Slots

THE PAYWALL is currently designed around a maximum of 21 slots.

Under the current pricing formula:

Slot #1  = €1
Slot #21 = €1,048,576

If all 21 slots are successfully purchased, the theoretical gross amount raised is:

€2,097,151

The project operator reserves the right to modify the number of slots or pricing structure before launch, provided that such changes are clearly communicated.

15. The 10% Community Allocation

THE PAYWALL intends to allocate 10% of the amount raised during the 30-day experiment to a community-selected outcome.

The community allocation is not an additional charge to purchasers.

29. Final Principle

THE PAYWALL exists to test one simple idea: how much would someone pay to become part of an internet experiment that gets more expensive every time someone joins?

The experiment lasts 30 days. Every successful purchase increases the price. And 10% of what the experiment raises is intended to go back to the community through the published voting mechanism.
